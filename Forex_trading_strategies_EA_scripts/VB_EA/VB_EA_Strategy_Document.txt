==========================================
  VB_EA - Strategy Document
  Volume Boundary Oscillator (VBO) Strategy
==========================================

==========================================
  1. STRATEGY OVERVIEW
==========================================

Name:           VB_EA (Volume Boundary Oscillator)
Type:           Volume-based Momentum / Mean Reversion
Version:        1.00
Symbol:         Any (designed for Forex majors)
Timeframe:      Any (recommended: M15, H1)
Direction:      Both (BUY and SELL)
Style:          Mechanical, fully automated

Description:
  The VB EA uses a custom volume oscillator that transforms tick volume
  through either a Butterfly Curve or Triple Sine function. When volume
  deviates significantly from its average and crosses a threshold, combined
  with price action confirmation, the EA enters trades.

  Unique feature: Alternating threshold logic for second entries — if the
  first entry was triggered at a negative threshold, the second entry
  triggers at a positive threshold (and vice versa).

==========================================
  2. INDICATOR: Volume Boundary Oscillator (VBO)
==========================================

  --- Step 1: Volume Statistics ---
    AvgVol = sum(volume[i]) / N          (N = VolumePeriod)
    StdVol = sqrt(sum(vol^2)/N - avg^2)  (population std dev)

  --- Step 2: Scale Volume into t ---

    Scaled Method (Z-Score):
      t = ScaleFactor * (currentVolume - AvgVol) / StdVol

    Logarithmic Method:
      t = ScaleFactor * log(currentVolume)

  --- Step 3: Apply Smoothing Function ---

    Butterfly Curve (range approximately +/-3.0):
      f(t) = sin(t) * (e^cos(t) - 2*cos(4t) - sin(t/12)^5)

      This creates a complex, multi-lobed oscillation pattern.
      The butterfly curve is sensitive to volume spikes and
      produces more varied signal amplitudes.

    Triple Sine (range +/-1.0):
      f(t) = sin(t)^3

      Simpler, bounded to [-1, +1]. Amplifies extremes,
      dampens mid-range noise. More predictable behavior.

==========================================
  3. ENTRY CONDITIONS
==========================================

  --- FIRST BUY ENTRY ---
    1. VBO crosses ABOVE BuyThreshold (+0.5 default)
       (previous bar VBO < threshold, current bar VBO >= threshold)
    2. Last closed candle is BULLISH (Close[1] > Open[1])
    3. Current open buy positions < 2
    4. Total open positions < 2

  --- FIRST SELL ENTRY ---
    1. VBO crosses BELOW SellThreshold (-0.5 default)
       (previous bar VBO > threshold, current bar VBO <= threshold)
    2. Last closed candle is BEARISH (Close[1] < Open[1])
    3. Current open sell positions < 2
    4. Total open positions < 2

  --- SECOND BUY ENTRY (Alternating Threshold) ---
    If the first buy triggered at a NEGATIVE threshold:
      → Second buy triggers when VBO crosses above POSITIVE threshold
    If the first buy triggered at a POSITIVE threshold:
      → Second buy triggers when VBO crosses above NEGATIVE threshold
    No price action confirmation required for second entry.

  --- SECOND SELL ENTRY (Alternating Threshold) ---
    Same alternating logic as buys but in reverse direction.
    No price action confirmation required for second entry.

==========================================
  4. EXIT CONDITIONS
==========================================

  All exits via fixed SL and TP in pips:

  BUY:  SL = Entry - StopLoss pips  |  TP = Entry + TakeProfit pips
  SELL: SL = Entry + StopLoss pips  |  TP = Entry - TakeProfit pips

  Default Risk-Reward = 100/50 = 2:1

  No signal-based exits. SL/TP handle all closing.

==========================================
  5. POSITION MANAGEMENT
==========================================

  Max BUY positions:   2
  Max SELL positions:  2
  Max TOTAL positions: 2 (buys + sells combined)
  Alternating Entry:   Second entry uses opposite threshold polarity
  Price Confirmation:  Required for first entry only
  Cooldown:            None (bar-based processing only)

==========================================
  8. SMOOTHING FUNCTION COMPARISON
==========================================

  Butterfly Curve:
    f(t) = sin(t) * (e^cos(t) - 2*cos(4t) - sin(t/12)^5)
    Range: approximately +/-3.0
    Behavior: Complex, multi-frequency oscillation
    Best for: Volatile markets, wider thresholds (e.g., +/-1.0)
    Pros: Captures volume spikes with nuance
    Cons: Less predictable output range

  Triple Sine:
    f(t) = sin(t)^3
    Range: exactly +/-1.0
    Behavior: Simple, smooth, bounded
    Best for: Stable markets, tighter thresholds (e.g., +/-0.5)
    Pros: Predictable, easy to set thresholds
    Cons: May miss subtle volume patterns
