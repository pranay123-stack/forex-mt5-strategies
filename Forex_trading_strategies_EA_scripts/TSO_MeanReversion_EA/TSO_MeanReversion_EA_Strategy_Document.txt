==========================================
  TSO_MeanReversion_EA - Strategy Document
  Triple Sine Oscillator Mean Reversion
==========================================

==========================================
  1. STRATEGY OVERVIEW
==========================================

Name:           TSO_MeanReversion_EA
Type:           Mean Reversion
Version:        1.00
Symbol:         Any (designed for Forex majors)
Timeframe:      Any (recommended: M15, H1)
Direction:      Both (BUY and SELL)
Style:          Mechanical, fully automated

Description:
  The TSO EA uses a custom oscillator based on the sine-cubed function
  applied to a Z-score of price. When price deviates significantly from
  its moving average (relative to standard deviation), the oscillator
  reaches extreme values. The EA enters trades expecting price to
  revert back toward the mean.

  Core idea: Buy when TSO crosses into oversold zone (< -0.7).
  Sell when TSO crosses into overbought zone (> +0.7).
  Multiple entries allowed in the same direction (up to MaxEntries).

==========================================
  2. INDICATOR: Triple Sine Oscillator (TSO)
==========================================

  --- Mathematical Formula ---

  Step 1: Compute Z-score
    scaled = Ksense * (Close - SMA) / StdDev

    Where:
      Close  = bar's close price
      SMA    = Simple Moving Average of Close (period = mPeriod)
      StdDev = Standard Deviation of Close (period = mPeriod)
      Ksense = sensitivity multiplier (default 0.5)

  Step 2: Apply sine cube
    TSO = sin(scaled)^3 = sin(scaled) * sin(scaled) * sin(scaled)

  --- Properties ---
    Range:       [-1, +1] (always bounded due to sin^3)
    > +0.7:      Overbought zone
    < -0.7:      Oversold zone
    ~ 0:         Near equilibrium (price near MA)

  --- Why Sine Cube? ---
    - sin() compresses any input to [-1, +1]
    - Cubing amplifies extreme values (near +/-1)
    - Cubing dampens mid-range noise (values near 0)
    - Preserves the sign (negative stays negative)
    - Creates sharper, more decisive signals at extremes

  --- Ksense Effect ---
    Ksense = 0.3: Less sensitive, fewer signals, larger moves needed
    Ksense = 0.5: Default, balanced sensitivity
    Ksense = 1.0: More sensitive, more signals, smaller moves trigger
    Ksense = 2.0: Very sensitive, many signals, risk of overtrading

==========================================
  3. ENTRY CONDITIONS
==========================================

  --- BUY ENTRY ---
    1. Previous bar TSO > -TSO_Threshold  (was NOT oversold)
    2. Current bar TSO  <= -TSO_Threshold  (just ENTERED oversold zone)
    3. No SELL positions open             (directional exclusivity)
    4. Total positions < MaxEntries        (capacity check)
    5. Bars since last entry >= BarInterval (cooldown)

    Logic: Price has dropped far enough below the mean that the TSO
    crossed into oversold territory. Expect a bounce back up.

  --- SELL ENTRY ---
    1. Previous bar TSO < +TSO_Threshold  (was NOT overbought)
    2. Current bar TSO  >= +TSO_Threshold  (just ENTERED overbought zone)
    3. No BUY positions open              (directional exclusivity)
    4. Total positions < MaxEntries        (capacity check)
    5. Bars since last entry >= BarInterval (cooldown)

    Logic: Price has risen far enough above the mean that the TSO
    crossed into overbought territory. Expect a pullback down.

==========================================
  4. EXIT CONDITIONS
==========================================

  Exits handled entirely by fixed SL and TP:

  BUY:  SL = Entry - StopLoss points  |  TP = Entry + TakeProfit points
  SELL: SL = Entry + StopLoss points  |  TP = Entry - TakeProfit points

  No manual close logic. SL/TP set at order open time.

  Default Risk-Reward = TP/SL = 200/100 = 2:1

==========================================
  5. POSITION MANAGEMENT
==========================================

  Directional Exclusivity:
    - If BUY positions are open → SELL signals are BLOCKED
    - If SELL positions are open → BUY signals are BLOCKED
    - Only one direction at a time

  Multiple Entries:
    - Up to MaxEntries (5) positions in the SAME direction
    - Each new signal can add another position if cooldown is met
    - Useful for pyramiding into a mean-reversion move

  Cooldown:
    - BarInterval (10) bars must pass between any entry
    - Prevents overtrading on rapid oscillations

  Max Positions: 5 total (buys + sells combined)

==========================================
  6. INPUT PARAMETERS
==========================================

  --- TSO Indicator ---
  Parameter        Default    Description
  ---------        -------    ---------------------------------
  mPeriod          20         SMA & StdDev lookback period
  Ksense           0.5        Sensitivity multiplier
  TSO_Threshold    0.7        Overbought/Oversold level

  --- Trade Settings ---
  Parameter        Default    Description
  ---------        -------    ---------------------------------
  TakeProfit       200        Take profit (points)
  StopLoss         100        Stop loss (points)
  LotSize          0.1        Lot size per entry
  MaxEntries       5          Max simultaneous positions
  BarInterval      10         Min bars between entries
  MagicNumber      12345      EA identifier

  NOTE: SL/TP are in POINTS (not pips).
    5-digit broker: 100 points = 10 pips
    4-digit broker: 100 points = 100 pips
