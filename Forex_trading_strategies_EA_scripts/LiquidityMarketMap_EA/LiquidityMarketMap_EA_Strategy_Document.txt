==========================================
  1. STRATEGY OVERVIEW
==========================================

Name:           LiquidityMarketMap_EA
Type:           Smart Money Concepts (SMC) / ICT-derived
Version:        1.00
Instrument:     NQ (Nasdaq Futures) or any symbol
Execution TF:   Chart timeframe (designed for 15s, works on any)
Direction:      Both (LONG and SHORT)
Style:          Precision execution within institutional zones

Core Premise:
  Markets move to collect liquidity. FVGs, iFVGs, Breaker Blocks,
  and Rejection Blocks mark levels where institutions have acted.
  Price is likely to return to these zones and react. By executing
  entries when price breaks through these zones, we capture
  high-probability reversal/continuation setups with tight risk.

==========================================
  2. ZONE TYPES (4 categories)
==========================================

  --- Fair Value Gap (FVG) ---
    3-candle formation with price gap between candle[0] and candle[2]
    Bullish: high[2] < low[0] → zone = [low[0], high[2]]
    Bearish: low[2] > high[0] → zone = [low[2], high[0]]
    Acts as magnet — price tends to return and fill the gap

  --- Inverse FVG (iFVG) — PRIMARY ENTRY ZONE ---
    Formed when price closes through an existing FVG, inverting polarity
    Bullish FVG closed through → becomes bearish iFVG (resistance)
    Bearish FVG closed through → becomes bullish iFVG (support)
    Highest conviction setup — tested, inverted, and defended by institutions

  --- Breaker Block ---
    Forms when price breaks through a prior swing high/low
    Broken resistance becomes support (bullish breaker)
    Broken support becomes resistance (bearish breaker)

  --- Rejection Block ---
    Large body candle (2x avg) closing in top/bottom 25% of range
    The wick area marks where price was aggressively rejected
    Bullish: lower wick = support | Bearish: upper wick = resistance

==========================================
  5. ENTRY LOGIC
==========================================

  Pre-entry checklist (automated):
    1. Session filter passes (NY or Asia)
    2. HTF bias determined from D1/H4 zones
    3. Zone detected and valid (iFVG preferred)
    4. Zone aligns with HTF bias direction
    5. Price enters zone and candle closes through

  LONG Entry:
    - Price retraces into a bullish zone (iFVG/FVG/Breaker/Rejection)
    - Last closed candle breaks above zone top
    - HTF bias is bullish (if RequireHTFBias enabled)

  SHORT Entry:
    - Price retraces into a bearish zone
    - Last closed candle breaks below zone bottom
    - HTF bias is bearish

  Zone priority: iFVG > FVG > Breaker > Rejection
  Higher TF zones prioritized over lower TF zones

==========================================
  6. STOP LOSS
==========================================

  LONG:  SL = previous swing low on execution TF (below entry)
  SHORT: SL = previous swing high on execution TF (above entry)

  Buffer of 2 points added beyond the swing level.

  Safety check: If SL distance > 1.5x average bar range,
  the setup is skipped (too far, invalid risk).

==========================================
  7. TAKE PROFIT (Priority Order)
==========================================

  1st: Previous structural swing high/low (HTF level)
  2nd: 0.9R (90% of risk distance) — disciplined default

==========================================
  8. POSITION SIZING
==========================================

  Risk-based sizing (not fixed lots):

    Contracts = Dollar Risk / (SL Distance in Points × Point Value)

  NQ: Point Value = $20
  ES: Point Value = $12.50
  Forex: Uses tick value automatically

  Example: $400 risk, 10-point SL → $400 / (10 × $20) = 2 contracts

==========================================
  9. RISK MANAGEMENT
==========================================

  - Max 2 active trades at once
  - Max 2 stop-outs per session (then stop trading)
  - Breakeven: move SL to entry + buffer when price moves 1R in favour
  - No revenge trading logic (enforced by session stop-out limit)
  - Position size recalculated per trade from SL distance

==========================================
  12. ZONE INVALIDATION RULES
==========================================

  A zone is invalidated when price CLOSES through it:
    - Bullish zone: candle closes below zone bottom → dead
    - Bearish zone: candle closes above zone top → dead
    - iFVG closed through again: zone is dead, no re-entry

  The EA automatically removes invalid zones each bar.
