==========================================
  ButterflyOscillatorEA - Strategy Document
  Butterfly Curve Peak/Trough Trading
==========================================

==========================================
  1. STRATEGY OVERVIEW
==========================================

Name:           ButterflyOscillatorEA
Type:           Trend-Following with Oscillator Timing
Version:        1.00
Symbol:         Any (designed for Forex majors)
Timeframe:      Any (recommended: M15, H1, H4)
Direction:      Both (BUY and SELL based on trend)
Style:          Mechanical, fully automated

Description:
  Uses the Butterfly Curve mathematical function as an oscillator
  to time entries within a trend defined by dual SMA crossover.
  Two selectable strategies: Peak entries (oscillator > +2.5) and
  Trough entries (oscillator < -2.5). Only one position at a time.

==========================================
  2. INDICATOR: Butterfly Oscillator
==========================================

  --- Formula (x-component of Butterfly Curve) ---

    x = sin(t) * (e^cos(t) - 2*cos(4t) - sin(t/12)^5)

  --- Step Size (t) ---

    Fixed Mode (UsePriceStep = false):
      tStep = tmStep (default 0.05)
      t = bar_index * tmStep

    Price Mode (UsePriceStep = true):
      tStep = MathMod((Close - Open) / _Point, tmStep)
      t = bar_index * tStep

  --- Output Range ---
    Approximately +/-3.0
    Upper threshold (peak):   +2.5
    Lower threshold (trough): -2.5

  --- Interpretation ---
    > +2.5:  Extreme high — momentum surge
    < -2.5:  Extreme low — momentum collapse
    ~0:      Neutral

==========================================
  3. TREND FILTER: Dual SMA
==========================================

  Fast SMA:  Period = 50 (default)
  Slow SMA:  Period = 200 (default)

  Fast > Slow → UPTREND
  Fast < Slow → DOWNTREND
  Fast ≈ Slow → NEUTRAL (no trades)

==========================================
  4. ENTRY CONDITIONS
==========================================

  --- Strategy 1: Peak (Upper Threshold +2.5) ---

    Trigger: Oscillator crosses ABOVE +2.5
      (prevOsc < 2.5 AND curOsc >= 2.5)

    + Uptrend (Fast MA > Slow MA) → OPEN BUY
    + Downtrend (Fast MA < Slow MA) → OPEN SELL

    Logic: A momentum surge in an uptrend confirms strength → buy.
    A momentum surge in a downtrend suggests exhaustion → sell.

  --- Strategy 2: Trough (Lower Threshold -2.5) ---

    Trigger: Oscillator crosses BELOW -2.5
      (prevOsc > -2.5 AND curOsc <= -2.5)

    + Uptrend → OPEN BUY (buy the dip)
    + Downtrend → OPEN SELL (momentum breakdown)

  --- Priority Rule ---
    When both strategies are enabled, whichever threshold is
    triggered first on a given bar takes priority. Strategy 1
    is checked before Strategy 2.

==========================================
  5. EXIT CONDITIONS
==========================================

  All exits via fixed SL and TP in POINTS:

  BUY:  SL = Entry - StopLoss points  |  TP = Entry + TakeProfit points
  SELL: SL = Entry + StopLoss points  |  TP = Entry - TakeProfit points

  SL or TP can be set to 0 to disable them.
  Default: SL = 500 pts, TP = 1000 pts (2:1 RR)

==========================================
  6. POSITION MANAGEMENT
==========================================

  Max Positions:     1 (only one open at a time)
  Direction:         Determined by trend at time of signal
  Multiple Entries:  No
  Pyramiding:        No
  Cooldown:          None (new bar check only)

==========================================
  9. STEP SIZE MODES EXPLAINED
==========================================

  Fixed Mode (UsePriceStep = false):
    t = bar_index * 0.05
    Bar 1: t = 0.05
    Bar 2: t = 0.10
    Bar 3: t = 0.15
    ...
    The oscillator cycles predictably through the butterfly curve.
    Good for: Consistent signal generation, easier to backtest.

  Price Mode (UsePriceStep = true):
    t = bar_index * MathMod((Close-Open)/_Point, tmStep)
    The step size varies based on candle body size.
    Larger candles = different t progression = different oscillator phase.
    Good for: Adaptive to market volatility, price-responsive signals.
